Our Story

A neighborhood place, quietly proud of its oven — ninety seconds at eight hundred degrees.

Frankie & Fanucci's in Mamaroneck, NY is more than a restaurant — it's the neighborhood's gathering place. Voted "Best of Westchester" six times, we've built our reputation on thin-crust perfection baked in traditional wood-fired ovens.

Our menu spans the full Italian table: handmade pastas, crispy antipasti, fresh salads, and entrées crafted with care. A full bar, handcrafted cocktails, and a family-friendly vibe make every visit memorable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers regarding dining, reservations, pickup, delivery, dietary options, and catering.

Yes, table reservations are available for both lunch and dinner. You can secure a table by calling (914) 630-4360 or visiting our Find Us page. Walk-in guests are also welcomed and seated as space permits.
We operate daily: Monday through Thursday from 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM, Friday from 12:00 PM to 10:00 PM, Saturday from 11:30 AM to 10:00 PM, and Sunday from 11:30 AM to 9:00 PM.
Dine-in guests can use street or municipal village parking near our main entrance at 301 Mamaroneck Avenue. For curbside pickup, drive to 300 Phillips Park Road at the rear of the building and call (914) 630-4360 upon arrival so our team can bring your order out to your vehicle immediately.
Yes. We offer vegetarian salads, pasta dishes, and specialty wood-fired pizzas including our Margherita, Shroom, Giambotta, and Popeye pies. Please notify your server or note any dietary restrictions when ordering so we can safely prepare your food.
We host celebrations, corporate gatherings, and private dinners for up to 40 guests. Parties of 10 or more select from structured family-style dining packages starting at $39.95 per person, with an optional table-side Craft-Your-Own Gelato Cart. Details and packages are available on our Groups & Events page.
We offer ½ size and full trays of pasta, Italian entrees, antipasti, and desserts designed to feed groups efficiently. We ask for 24-hour advance notice to ensure proper preparation. View items and pricing on our Catering page or submit an order directly online.
Delivery is available daily from 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM for a $2.95 fee. We service Mamaroneck, Larchmont, Harrison (south of I-95), Rye (south of Osborn Rd / Oakland Beach Ave), Scarsdale (east of Bronx River Pkwy), and specified sections of New Rochelle. Full zone boundaries can be viewed on our Delivery & Pickup page.
Our 2-for-1 Happy Hour is available all day, every day at the bar. Enjoy 2-for-1 pricing across signature cocktails, select wines, and draft beers alongside a dedicated bar snack menu. See full drink listings on our Happy Hour page.
