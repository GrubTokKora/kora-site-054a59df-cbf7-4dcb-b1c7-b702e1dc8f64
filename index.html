Our Story

A neighborhood place, quietly proud of its oven — ninety seconds at eight hundred degrees.

Frankie & Fanucci's in Mamaroneck, NY is more than a restaurant — it's the neighborhood's gathering place. Voted "Best of Westchester" six times, we've built our reputation on thin-crust perfection baked in traditional wood-fired ovens.

Our menu spans the full Italian table: handmade pastas, crispy antipasti, fresh salads, and entrées crafted with care. A full bar, handcrafted cocktails, and a family-friendly vibe make every visit memorable.

Good to Know

Quick answers to common questions about dining in, takeout, reservations, catering, and our scratch kitchen.

Yes, we gladly welcome reservations for dining in! You can reserve your table by calling us directly at (914) 630-4360 or visiting our Find Us page. Walk-in guests are always warmly welcomed on a first-come, first-served basis.
We are open 7 days a week: Monday through Thursday from 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM, Friday from 12:00 PM to 10:00 PM, Saturday from 11:30 AM to 10:00 PM, and Sunday from 11:30 AM to 9:00 PM.
Convenient street parking is available along Mamaroneck Avenue, and several municipal parking lots are located within a short 1- to 2-minute walk from our front door at 301 Mamaroneck Avenue.
Yes! Our menu features vegetarian specialties including wood-fired pizzas (such as our Margherita, Popeye, and White pizza), crisp salads, and antipasti. We accommodate food allergies and dietary preferences—just let our team know when you order so we can prepare your dish with care.
We love celebrations! We host private and semi-private events for up to 40 guests, perfect for birthdays, sports banquets, family gatherings, and corporate dinners. We offer 3 curated per-person event packages. Explore our Group Dining packages or reach out to reserve your date.
Our catering menu offers wood-fired pizzas, hearty pasta trays, fresh salads, and Italian classics sold by the tray. We serve Mamaroneck, Larchmont, Harrison, Rye, Scarsdale, and New Rochelle. Check our Catering Menu & Guide to plan for your gathering.
You can order directly online for fast in-store pickup, curbside, or home delivery through our Online Ordering system. Learn more about zones and hours on our Delivery & Pickup page.
Our 2-for-1 Happy Hour runs all day, every day at the bar! Enjoy buy-one-get-one on handcrafted cocktails, wines by the glass, and draft beers. See all current pours on our Happy Hour page.
